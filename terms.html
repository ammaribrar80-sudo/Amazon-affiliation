The Method

Tested before
shelved.

Three rules. No exceptions. They are the only reason this page is worth reading.

01

Three weeks in real hands.

Every device we carry spends twenty-one days in daily use before it earns a place. If it breaks, feels cheap, or underperforms, it never reaches the page.

02

Nothing decorative.

We strip away the noise. What remains is a short list of instruments that do one thing extremely well — and never ask for more attention than they give.

03

Honest, always.

No sponsored placements. No paid rankings. Our only income is the small affiliate commission Amazon pays us when you buy — at no extra cost to you.

Questions

Before you buy.

How do you make money from this site?
Kage is a participant in the Amazon Associates programme. When you click through and buy, Amazon pays us a small commission. The price you pay is exactly the same. We do not accept payment for placement, and no brand can buy a spot on the shelf.
Why do prices differ between the India and US versions?
Each Amazon marketplace sets its own pricing, taxes and stock. Our collection currently shows Indian pricing for Amazon.in; if you are shopping from another region, the Amazon page will usually offer a regional equivalent once you open it.
How often is the collection updated?
Stock, pricing and ratings are reviewed monthly. A product is removed when it is discontinued, repeatedly out of stock, or beaten clearly by something newer we have tested.
Do you accept free review units?
No. Every unit is bought at retail price. It is slower and more expensive, but it means we owe nobody a favourable verdict.
A product is out of stock — what now?
Amazon usually shows the next available seller or a restock date on the product page. If a piece has been unavailable for more than sixty days, it is removed from the shelf.
