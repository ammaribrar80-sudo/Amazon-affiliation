Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

This policy explains what information Kage (“we”, “us”) collects when you visit this website, why we collect it, and what you can do about it. We keep it deliberately short and plain.

1. Who we are

Kage is an independent product review and affiliate website. We publish articles and curated product lists and link to Amazon, where you may choose to buy.

2. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not sell products directly. The information we may process falls into three groups:

  • Analytics data — pages viewed, approximate location (country/city level), device type, browser, referring website, and time on page. This is aggregated and is not used to identify you.
  • Technical data — your IP address and standard server logs, retained by our hosting provider for security and abuse prevention.
  • Voluntary information — anything you send us, for example if you email us a question.

3. Cookies and local storage

Cookies are small files stored by your browser. We use them for two purposes:

  • Preference storage — remembering which Amazon marketplace (India or US) you selected, so prices stay in your currency on your next visit.
  • Affiliate tracking — Amazon sets cookies when you click one of our links, so that a purchase can be attributed to us. We earn a commission at no extra cost to you.

You can clear or block cookies in your browser settings at any time. Blocking them will not prevent you from using this site.

4. Amazon Associates

Kage participates in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program and the Amazon India Associates Programme. When you click an outbound product link, you leave our site and become subject to Amazon's own privacy notice and cookie policy. Please review those before purchasing.

In short: we do not collect your name, address, or payment details. Amazon handles the transaction, and we only ever see anonymous, aggregated reports of clicks and commissions.

5. How we use information

  • To understand which pages and products are useful, so we can improve the site.
  • To keep the site secure and prevent automated abuse.
  • To measure affiliate performance in aggregate.

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information. We do not run behaviourally targeted advertising of our own.

6. Third-party services

We rely on a small number of external providers, each with its own privacy policy: our web host, an analytics provider, Google Fonts (font delivery), and Amazon (affiliate links). Images on this site are loaded from Unsplash until replaced with product photography.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you, or to object to its processing. Because we hold very little identifiable data, most requests are simple to fulfil — email us and we will respond within 30 days.

If you are in the EEA or UK, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

8. Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

9. Data retention and security

Aggregated analytics are retained for up to 26 months. Server logs are kept for a short period for security purposes. All traffic is served over HTTPS.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the “last updated” date above will change and the revised policy will appear on this page.
